## First License Transition

Commit `a8c38158ae3f51f9511b721640ab7250692901fe` changed the repository by an
ordinary commit from the AGPL-3.0 text to the historical WZRD.VID custom
source-available license. That transition occurred before the `v0.1.0` tag.

The custom terms permitted specified personal and noncommercial uses while
reserving redistribution, commercial use, and branding rights. Commit
`08a1e2de57e8e31b4e77cf8a9fbc6b4917014f68` later clarified those terms. Rights
validly granted for copies under either historical custom text remain effective

documentation, legal, Pages, support, and distribution surface, while current
