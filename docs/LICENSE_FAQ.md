This is a plain-language summary. The `LICENSE` file is the controlling WZRD.VID Freeware License.

Yes. Copies that include the WZRD.VID Freeware License may be installed and used without charge. Official desktop packaged downloads are temporarily unavailable; this availability change does not alter the license for an existing copy.

## Can I use it for work?

Yes. Professional use is permitted without a separate commercial-use license.

## Can my company use it?

Yes. Businesses, agencies, studios, production houses, nonprofits, and other organizations may use WZRD.VID internally and for their work.

## Can I make paid client work with it?

Yes. Freelance work, paid client projects, and other commercial creative services are permitted.

Yes. You may publish, sell, license, and monetize videos, images, audio, and other media you create with WZRD.VID, assuming you have the necessary rights in your source material.

No. Merely importing, processing, or rendering media with WZRD.VID does not transfer ownership of your media, project files, or output to AMPYX LLC.

No. You remain responsible for rights, permissions, licenses, and clearances for video, images, audio, performances, trademarks, or other material you use.

Not without separate written authorization from AMPYX LLC. Charging for creative work made with WZRD.VID is permitted; charging others for copies of WZRD.VID itself is not.

## Can I redistribute WZRD.VID?

Only where the controlling license, an applicable third-party license, or a separate written agreement expressly permits it. Linking people to the official website or release records is fine; no official desktop binary download is currently available.

Not for the AMPYX-owned proprietary portions without separate authorization. If source accompanies your copy, private personal or internal-business modification is permitted, but distributing source or modified builds is not.

Not AMPYX-owned proprietary code without separate permission. This does not restrict your commercial media output or your use of separately licensed third-party components under their own terms.

Not without separate written authorization from AMPYX LLC. Official direct-download or App Store distribution by AMPYX is a separate matter from using WZRD.VID for commercial work.

No. WZRD.VID is proprietary freeware. Availability of historical source code or the current public documentation/site tree does not convert AMPYX-owned portions into open-source software.

Historical binary assets have been withdrawn, but their source tags, release records, and prior grants remain preserved. See `docs/LICENSE_HISTORY.md`.

Not without arranging it with AMPYX LLC in advance. Current desktop development is private. Bug reports, feature requests, issues, and discussion are welcome, but unsolicited outside code pull requests are not accepted by default.
