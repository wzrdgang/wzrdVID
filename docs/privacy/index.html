//// PRIVACY SUMMARY

Local media stays local.

WZRD.VID Lite is a local media tool. It lets you choose media from your device, render a short stylized clip, and save the result to Photos or your browser download destination.

NO ACCOUNT NO ANALYTICS NO TRACKING
DATA

What we do not collect

WZRD.VID Lite does not require an account, does not upload your media, does not collect analytics, does not use tracking, and does not sell personal information.

  • no sign-in
  • no backend upload
  • no tracking SDKs
MEDIA

How media is handled

User-selected photos, videos, GIFs, and audio are processed on the device. Output is saved to Photos only when you tap Download in the Apple Lite wrapper, or handled by browser download behavior in the web Lite version.

After output is saved, you decide whether to keep, delete, or share it.

ACCESS

Photos and files

WZRD.VID Lite asks for Photos or file access so you can select local media and save rendered clips. The app may temporarily write a rendered clip on your device so it can validate and save the video to Photos.

LOCAL PREF

Language preference

WZRD.VID Lite may store your interface language preference locally in the app browser environment. This preference is not uploaded to AMPYX LLC.

CHANGES

Future changes

If WZRD.VID Lite changes to collect data in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy information should be updated before release.

CONTACT

Contact

AMPYX LLC support contact details will be finalized before App Store submission. Current WZRD.VID Lite support notes are available on the support page.
