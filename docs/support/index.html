Local media in, short clip out.

WZRD.VID Lite turns user-selected photos, videos, GIFs, and optional audio into short stylized clips. The Lite workflow is local-first: files stay on the device and are not uploaded to AMPYX LLC.

NO SIGN-IN NO UPLOADS PHOTOS SAVE
IMPORT

How do I add media?

Use Add Media to choose local photos, videos, or GIFs. Use Add Audio if you want a separate local audio file. Lite depends on browser and iOS media support, so the desktop app still supports more formats.

  • local files only
  • no account required
  • no media upload path
RENDER

How do I make a clip?

Pick a 15, 30, or 60 second duration, choose a preset and quality setting, optionally enable Random clip assembly, then tap Render. The clip is generated on the device.

Lite is not full desktop WZRD.VID parity. The desktop app remains the full renderer for broader media formats, deeper controls, and longer workflows.

EXPORT

How do I save output?

After rendering finishes, tap Download. In the Apple Lite wrapper, the rendered video is validated locally and saved to Photos. In the browser version, the browser download behavior handles the rendered file.

You decide whether to keep, delete, or share the saved output after it leaves WZRD.VID Lite.

PHOTOS

Why does Lite ask for Photos access?

Photos access is used for user-directed actions: selecting local photos or videos and saving the rendered clip when you tap Download. The app does not upload your media and does not browse your Photos library for analytics or tracking.

LIMITS

What is not included yet?

Source clip audio from visual timeline media is future work in Lite. Explicit Add Audio is supported, but Lite does not currently preserve source audio from the selected visual media timeline.
